<h2 align="center">About Me</h2>

<p align="center">
  I am a <b>DPhil researcher at the University of Oxford</b>, within the
  <b>Visual Geometry Group (VGG)</b>, supervised by
  <b>Professor Andrea Vedaldi</b>.
  My research focuses on <b>Spatial AI, 3D computer vision, and
  controllable 3D reconstruction</b>, particularly learning
  representations for geometric and physical reasoning.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Previously, I worked as a <b>Machine Learning Engineer at
  Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team</b>, researching and
  developing deep learning methods for aerodynamic modelling,
  computational geometry, and scientific simulation.
  My work included <b>transformer architectures for unstructured
  3D meshes and point clouds</b>, aerodynamic prediction,
  inverse design, and GPU-accelerated simulation infrastructure
  using <b>CUDA, C++, PyTorch, and NVIDIA Warp</b>.
</p>

<p align="center">
  My background also includes <b>software engineering, architecture,
  visual effects, procedural generation, and computational design</b>.
  My broader interests include computer graphics, robotics,
  world models, and high-performance machine learning systems.
</p>
